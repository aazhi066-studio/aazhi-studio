# v10 Vatapi (Pallava revenge, 642 CE) - ART DIRECTION "Raking-Light Stone Relief" (new to the channel)
Story: c.618-19 Chalukya king Pulakeshin II marches south toward Kanchi; meets Pallava king Mahendravarman at Pullalur near Kanchi; outcome disputed (Chalukya inscriptions claim victory, some historians read Pallava records the other way); most accounts: Kanchi held, northern lands lost. Son Narasimhavarman I on the throne c.630. c.642 strikes back, accounts say beats Pulakeshin near Kanchi, marches on Vatapi (Badami); Pulakeshin believed killed; Vatapi taken, said to be burned; title VATAPIKONDA; Pallavas held the city ~12 years (some accounts). TRADITION: general Paranjothi, later a saint; brought home a Ganesha statue = Vatapi Ganapati (LEGEND, not record). Today Badami = red sandstone cave temples; the fire rarely told.
Script: script.json; line times data/lines.json; word times data/words.json (L(i), Le(i), Wd(word, line) from src/core/timing.js).
Look: the whole film is a sequence of CARVED STONE RELIEF PANELS (like a temple frieze) lit by a moving low sun. Every figure, city, army and map is a low-relief shape raised from a stone slab; the light direction moves, so bevel highlights and cast shadows slide across the carving.
 - Two stones: PALLAVA = cool grey granite (#8C8A85 base, light #C9C5BC, shadow #3F3D3A, speckle of black/white mica). CHALUKYA / VATAPI = red sandstone (#A4553A base, light #D88A62, shadow #5A2A1C, soft horizontal bedding stripes). Fire = molten orange light #FF8A2A / #FFD27A glowing ON the stone (additive glow, reflected light on bevels). Night = deep blue-black #12161E with stone lit only by fire or moon.
 - RELIEF RENDER (the core trick): draw each shape as a Path2D; render it 3 times: dark shadow copy offset along -lightDir by 6-10 px, light rim copy offset +lightDir by 2-3 px, then the face fill. lightDir animates over the shot (raking light sweep). Cache stone textures (noise speckle) once per stone; clip texture into faces. No shadowBlur per path (cost!). Optional: one cached soft cast-shadow layer per shot.
 - Figures: faceless, stylised relief figures (Pallava/Mamallapuram-like elongated slender bodies: tall crowns as simple tapered shapes, no facial features). Pulakeshin = red-sandstone king silhouette with a boar-banner pennant (Chalukya varaha emblem, simple boar shape). Pallava = grey granite with a lion banner (simple maned lion shape). Narasimhavarman = granite king with tall crown and sword. Paranjothi = general silhouette labelled TRADITION. Ganesha statue = small simple relief icon (rounded head + trunk curve), labelled LEGEND. Elephants, horses, war chariots, marching rows as repeated relief units.
 - Devices NEW to the channel (use several per shot):
   (1) RAKING LIGHT SWEEP: light angle sweeps across the panel; a bright sun disc / beam band slides over the stone; highlights + shadows flip.
   (2) CHISEL CHIP REVEAL: a shape is cut into the stone with flying stone chips (small particles bursting from a moving chisel point) and a dust puff; outline appears progressively.
   (3) FRIEZE TRACK: the camera trucks sideways along one long continuous carved panel (a procession/army/march) - very wide cached panel, pan across.
   (4) SLAB SLIDE: heavy stone slabs slide in from the side / drop from above with a thud and dust to change shots (dust skirt at the base).
   (5) FIRE LIGHT ON STONE: orange flicker light from below; carved flames as relief; drifting sparks and smoke wisps over stone.
   (6) BRONZE PLAQUE LABELS: small bevelled bronze plate (#8A6A2E to #D9B465, dark engraved text) bolted on with 2 rivets, slides/pops in: "RECORD", "TRADITION", "LEGEND", "ACCOUNTS SAY", "DISPUTED", "ILLUSTRATION", dates.
   (7) EROSION / WEATHERING: a carving softens and fades (time passes), lichen-green spots creep in, sand blows across.
   (8) RELIEF MAP: the Deccan + Tamil country carved as a raised granite landmass on a sunk sea; routes as carved channels that light up with fire-orange as an army moves (Kanchi in the south-east, Vatapi/Badami north-west, ~arrow north-west).
 - Text: big words Inter 900 caps, CARVED into stone (draw dark inner shadow copy offset +2,+3, then face slightly darker than stone, light lip offset -1,-1) or on bronze plaques. Small notes Caladea 700. NO Tamil/Sanskrit script text; no invented inscription text (an inscription may appear only as abstract unreadable carved lines/rows of nicks, labelled RECORD).
 - Never (used before): blueprint/cyanotype, rubber stamps, balance scales, palm-leaf bundles, compass roses, measuring tapes (v5); shadow puppets, crack transitions (v6); lanterns going out (v4); sand pours, strata, pearls, glass bottle (v7); kalamkari cloth, stitches, dye drips, fold/unfold (v8); linocut, gouge carving on black block, ink roller, paper burn, ribbon banners, print pull (v9).
 - Captions are drawn by the engine at y ~1400-1640; keep y 1300-1760 calm (no big text there). Main action in y 150-1250.
 - Every shot: camera always moving (sh.zoom, sh.cam), something animates the whole time. Shots 1.5-4 s. Draw cost < ~0.2 s/frame: no per-path shadowBlur/filter; cache static layers with cached()/offscreen canvases; textures generated once.
 - Violence: no gore. Pulakeshin's death = his boar banner falling / a sandstone crown toppling, labelled BELIEVED. The burning = fire light and carved flames over sandstone city walls and roofs, smoke.
Engine: src/main.js (shot = {id, at, draw(lt, d, t), cam:[x,y], zoom, note}), src/core/lib.js (ctx, W=1080, H=1920, clamp, lerp, seg, eo, eio, eob, rnd, TAU, circle, ell, rr, line), src/core/timing.js (L, Le, Wd), src/art.js (lg, poly, fillP, rgba, mixc, cached(key,w,h,q,fn), blit).
