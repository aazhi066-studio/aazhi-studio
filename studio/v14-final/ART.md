Story (hedged, matches desc.txt): c.1656 Madurai (Tirumala Nayak) vs Mysore (Kanthirava Narasaraja I). Madurai had sided with Mysore's enemy, the Sultan of Bijapur; when Bijapur's army went home, Mysore struck back. Mysore army marched south under general (Dalavayi) Hampaiya. OLD ACCOUNTS SAY his soldiers cut off captives' noses, even villagers; THEY SAY the noses were sent in sacks to Srirangapatna as trophies. Historians read it as punishment, humiliation, perhaps ritual. Tirumala, old and ill, called ally Raghunatha Sethupathi of Ramnad; the Sethupathi's army, SAID TO BE 25,000, drove the invaders back. Madurai's army marched into Mysore country and ACCOUNTS SAY hunted noses too. One Madurai account claims the Mysore king lost his own nose = LEGEND. Tirumala died c.1659. The wars went on for years; the episode is mostly forgotten; the old name "the hunt for noses".
