# v9 Kuyili & Velu Nachiyar - ART DIRECTION "Ember Linocut" (new to the channel)
Story: 1772 Company + Nawab of Arcot troops attack Kalaiyar Kovil, king Muthu Vaduganatha killed; queen Velu Nachiyar escapes with daughter Vellachi; ~8 years near Dindigul under Hyder Ali's protection (accounts say he lent soldiers + guns); c.1780 returns with the Maruthu brothers, retakes Sivaganga, rules as queen (RECORD). Kuyili's gunpowder-store fire attack = FOLK SONG / ORAL TRADITION, evidence scarce, details vary. Memorials planned in Sivaganga district. Script: script.json; line times data/lines.json; word times data/words.json (L(i), Le(i), Wd(word, line) from src/core/timing.js).
Look: the whole film is a hand-carved LINOCUT / woodblock print series coming alive: bold black carved shapes on warm rough paper, gouge-mark textures, a second ink colour printed slightly off-register, and fire.
 - Palette: paper #EADBC0 (shade #D6C29E), carving black #141010, vermilion print ink #D2401F (dark #8E2412), ember orange #F39A2B, soot grey #4A4440, cold Company slate-blue #3E5872 (ONLY for Company troops/redcoat-free silhouettes; no uniforms detail). Signal colour for key labels: paper-cut ribbon banner (vermilion ribbon, paper text). Night/fire shots: black page with ember orange + vermilion carved light.
 - Texture: paper fibre grain (cached tile of faint speckles) over every shot; inside big black areas, parallel GOUGE LINES (thin paper-coloured curved strokes, like a V-gouge cut) following the form (cached per shape).
 - Figures: faceless carved silhouettes in profile, chunky woodcut shapes (no eyes/mouth/features), long-limbed. Velu Nachiyar = woman with sword and a high hair knot, sometimes on horseback; Vellachi = small girl figure; Kuyili = woman figure (never a portrait, often shown only as a silhouette inside flame, labelled FOLK SONG). Company troops = slate-blue rows of identical silhouettes with muskets/bayonets. Hyder Ali = a turbaned silhouette seen from behind or in shadow, never a face. Maruthu brothers = two matching silhouettes.
 - Devices NEW to the channel (use several per shot):
   (1) GOUGE CARVE REVEAL: a shape appears as if carved: thin paper-coloured gouge strokes sweep across a black block and the image emerges (clip + many short curved strokes revealed progressively).
   (2) INK ROLLER WIPE: a cylinder roller (drawn: handle + roller with highlight) rolls across the frame leaving a fresh ink layer = transition into the next shot.
   (3) OFF-REGISTER PRINT: the vermilion layer is printed with a small offset that jitters/settles (2-8 px) - print shake on beats.
   (4) PAPER BURN: a burning hole with glowing ember rim and charred edge eats through the paper to reveal the next scene (radial noisy mask, orange rim). Use for the fire beats.
   (5) RISING EMBERS + HEAT SHIMMER on fire shots (small particle sparks drifting up; cheap).
   (6) PAPER-CUT RIBBON BANNERS for labels: vermilion folded ribbon with forked ends and a paper fold shadow: "RECORD", "FOLK SONG", "ACCOUNTS SAY", "UNCERTAIN", "ILLUSTRATION", dates. Ribbon unfurls from the left.
   (7) PRINT PULL: a new print sheet is lifted/peeled from the block (corner curl reveal, paper back shown light) to change shots.
   (8) CARVED MAP: southern Tamil country as a carved block (black land, paper sea with wavy gouge lines), routes as carved chevron trails.
 - Text: big words Inter 900 caps, carving black on paper (or paper on black), slight off-register vermilion copy behind (4px). Small notes Caladea 700. NO Tamil script text; no invented quotes/inscriptions/letters/documents with text.
 - Never (used before): blueprint/cyanotype, rubber stamps, balance scales, palm-leaf bundles, compass roses, measuring tapes, dimension lines (v5); shadow puppets, crack transitions (v6); lanterns going out (v4); sand pours, strata, pearls, glass bottle (v7); kalamkari cloth, cloth registers, running stitches, dye drips, embroidered patches, fold/unfold (v8).
 - Captions are drawn by the engine at y ~1400-1640; keep y 1300-1760 calm (no big text there). Main action in y 150-1250.
 - Every shot: camera always moving (sh.zoom, sh.cam), something animates the whole time. Shots 1.5-4 s. Draw cost < ~0.2 s/frame: no per-path shadowBlur/filter; cache static layers with cached()/offscreen canvases; textures generated once.
 - Violence: no gore. The king's death = a carved crown falling / a banner dropping. Kuyili's fire = a flame silhouette and a flash, no burning body detail.
Engine: src/main.js (shot = {id, at, draw(lt, d, t), cam:[x,y], zoom, note}), src/core/lib.js (ctx, W=1080, H=1920, clamp, lerp, seg, eo, eio, eob, rnd, TAU, circle, ell, rr, line), src/core/timing.js (L, Le, Wd), src/art.js (lg, poly, fillP, rgba, mixc, cached(key,w,h,q,fn), blit).
 Sound cues: cue(t, type, {gain, dur, pan}); types: whoosh slide pop plop gulp boing bubbles sizzle sparkle thud slam tock zip alarm inhale heartbeat drum rumble chisel bell swell.
