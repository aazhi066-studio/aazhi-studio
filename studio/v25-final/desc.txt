In 1619 the Tamil kingdom of Jaffna, in the north of Sri Lanka, came to an end. Its last king, Cankili II (Cankili Kumaran), had taken power around 1617; accounts say he did it by killing rival claimants and princes of royal blood. He resisted the Portuguese and brought in soldiers from the Thanjavur Nayaks. In 1619 a Portuguese force under Phillippe de Oliveira took Nallur. Cankili tried to escape by boat to South India, but accounts say strong winds forced him back, and he was captured with his family and taken to Goa. Some histories say he was hanged there, others that he was beheaded, and the year is given as 1619 or 1621. The surviving royals, accounts say, were told to take holy orders as monks and nuns. 2. Portuguese conquest of the Jaffna kingdom (Wikipedia) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_conquest_of_the_Jaffna_kingdom

3. Phillippe de Oliveira (Wikipedia) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillippe_de_Oliveira
   Supports: Oliveira as the Portuguese commander who ousted Cankili II in 1619 and the start of Portuguese rule in Jaffna.

   Supports: the Tamil kingdom of Jaffna with its capital at Nallur, and its end in 1619.
5. T. Abeysinghe, Jaffna Under the Portuguese (Lake House, 1986), the standard scholarly account cited by the articles above.

WHAT IS ILLUSTRATION
Everything on screen is drawn as an old sea chart: the coastlines are stylised and not to scale. The king, soldiers, family and monks are faceless figures, not portraits. The fort stands for the royal capital at Nallur. The rope and the blade are symbols for the two versions of his death; nothing shown is a real image or a real document.

WHAT IS UNCERTAIN
- How Cankili took power comes from accounts written largely from the Portuguese side; how far he was a usurper or a defender of Jaffna is debated.
- Whether he was hanged or beheaded in Goa differs between sources.
- The year of his execution is given as 1619 in some accounts and 1621 in others.
- The winds that drove his boat back, and the fate of the surviving royals, come from those accounts and cannot be checked independently.
