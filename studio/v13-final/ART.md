# v13 Vellore Mutiny, 10 July 1806 - ART DIRECTION "Paper Theatre" (new to the channel)
Story (hedged, matches desc.txt): 51 years before 1857, sepoys of the East India Company's Madras army rose at Vellore Fort (Tamil Nadu) on the night of 10 July 1806. Trigger: Nov 1805 dress orders banned forehead marks and ordered beards shaved; a new round hat with a badge (cockade) RUMOURED to be leather from animals the soldiers would never touch; men who refused were punished - ACCOUNTS SAY two were flogged and dismissed. Tipu Sultan's family was held in the fort since his death in 1799; a daughter's wedding the day before brought many guests in. A little after 2 am sepoys struck; many British soldiers of the 69th Regiment were killed as they slept; ACCOUNTS COUNT 14 officers + about 115 men dead; the flag of Mysore was raised over the fort. Word got out to the British cavalry at Arcot (~25 km). Col. Rollo Gillespie rode ahead with a few horsemen; then guns came and blasted open the gate. Within hours crushed; ~350 sepoys killed; ~100 SAID to be shot without trial. After trials some were tied to cannon and blown away, others shot or hanged (show ONLY abstractly: cannon silhouette + white flash/smoke, a row of empty posts, no bodies/gore). Dress orders withdrawn. Governor of Madras (Bentinck) + army chief (Cradock) recalled; Tipu's family sent to Calcutta; whether they planned it is NOT CLEAR. Fort still stands; mostly forgotten.
