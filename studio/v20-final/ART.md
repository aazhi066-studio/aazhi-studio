Story (hedged, matches desc.txt): in the 1750s Puli Thevar, palayakkarar of Nerkattumseval (south Tamil country), refused tribute to the Nawab of Arcot and the British East India Company. Accounts say Yusuf Khan (the Company's commander) battered his fort with cannon for weeks; in 1761 it fell. Puli Thevar slipped away.
