# v11 Muziris (lost Chera port that traded with Rome) - ART DIRECTION "Living Mosaic" (new to the channel)
Story (hedged, matches desc.txt): ~2,000 years ago Muziris (Tamil: Muciri), a port of the Chera kings on the Malabar coast near the Periyar river mouth, traded with the Roman world. Sangam poem (Akananuru 149, PARAPHRASE only) pictures Yavana (foreign, western) ships arriving with gold, leaving with pepper. Pliny the Elder: nearest market of India, pirates nearby, ships anchor far out and cargo is ferried by small boats. Muziris papyrus (Vienna, 2nd c. CE): a loan contract for a voyage to Muziris + the cargo of the ship Hermapollon, valued at millions of drachmas; one study (De Romanis) argues most value was pepper. Tabula Peutingeriana (a MEDIEVAL COPY of a Roman map) marks a "Templum Augusti" near Muziris. Then records go quiet; nobody knows exactly how it faded. POPULAR THEORY: Periyar flood of 1341 reshaped the coast and helped Kochi's harbour rise. 2007: excavations at Pattanam village near the old river mouth: 1000+ Roman amphora sherds, traces of a possible wharf. Many scholars think Pattanam may be Muziris; not everyone agrees.
Script: script.json; line times data/lines.json; word times data/words.json (L(i), Le(i), Wd(word, line) from src/core/timing.js).

Look: the whole film is a ROMAN-STYLE FLOOR MOSAIC that builds, ripples and breaks apart. Every scene is made of small square tesserae (tile ~18-26 px, 2-3 px dark grout gaps, each tile slightly jittered in size/angle/shade by rnd). Background = cream marble tesserae field (#E9DFC9 / #D8CBB0 / #F4EEDF mix) with a guilloche/meander border option.
 - Palette: lapis sea #1F4E8C / #2E6DB4 / #7FB2E0 (wave crests white #F4F1E8); terracotta #B5552F / #D9794A; pepper black #232020 with green-black #2E3A24 vines; Roman gold smalti #D9A63A / #F3CF6B (glints = a tile flashing bright); Chera bow-banner red #9E2A2B; flood mud ochre #8C6A3A / #B08A52; jungle/field green #4E7A3A / #7FA65A. Grout #2A2622.
 - RENDER (the core trick, keep it cheap): build each picture as a grid of tile colours sampled from simple vector shapes (draw the shapes once onto a small offscreen canvas, read pixels with getImageData, ONE tile per cell), then draw all tiles once into a cached canvas. Animate by blitting the cached layer + drawing only the few hundred MOVING tiles per frame. Never draw >2500 individual tiles per frame. No shadowBlur/filter per tile.
 - Devices NEW to the channel (use several per shot):
   (1) TILE ASSEMBLY: tiles fly in from scattered positions (or rain from above) and click into place, picture builds row by row / radially; small 'tock' cues.
   (2) TILE RIPPLE: water areas = tiles whose shade cycles in travelling wave bands (per-tile colour by sin(x*k - t)), plus a gentle per-tile lift/offset.
   (3) MISSING-TILE LOSS: tiles pop out and fall away leaving bare grey mortar bed (#6E6A62 with trowel scratches) = things being lost / forgotten / unknown. Holes in the picture = uncertainty.
   (4) SMALTI GLINT: a diagonal light sweep across gold tiles; single tiles twinkle.
   (5) TESSERAE COUNTERS: numbers and dates spelled in big mosaic digits (5x7 tile font) that assemble; e.g. 2,000 YEARS, 1341, 2007, 1,000+.
   (6) MOSAIC MAP: Indian Ocean map in tiles: Red Sea/Egypt (Berenike) top-left, Malabar coast bottom-right; route = a line of gold tiles laying itself tile by tile; monsoon wind = rows of light-blue tiles flowing.
   (7) TILE TRANSITION: the whole frame flips like tiles turning over (each tile scales x 1->0->1 with a diagonal delay, revealing the next picture) - use between some shots.
   (8) MUD SWAMP: ochre flood tiles spread over a picture, covering it tile by tile (the 1341 flood / burial under fields).
 - LABELS: rectangular marble tablet tiles (#F4EEDF, thin dark border, Caladea 700 caps text in #2A2622) that drop in: "RECORD", "POEM (PARAPHRASE)", "THEORY", "UNCERTAIN", "ILLUSTRATION", "MEDIEVAL COPY", "ONE STUDY", "MAY BE", dates. Big words: Inter 900 caps in dark grout colour on marble tablet, or mosaic letters.
 - Figures: faceless simple tile silhouettes (no portraits). Pliny = generic Roman figure with toga + scroll, labelled ILLUSTRATION. Yavana ships = Roman merchant ship (one square sail, curved stern). Chera emblem = a BOW (with arrow) on red banner. Pepper = vines with clusters of black/green round berries; sacks; amphorae (tall two-handled jars with pointed base). Gold = coins as round gold tile discs. Papyrus = a sheet with abstract unreadable rows of short dark tile dashes (NO readable Greek text, no invented text), labelled RECORD. Peutinger map = long strip with abstract roads and a small temple icon labelled TEMPLE OF AUGUSTUS (label is fine; no fake Latin).
 - Never (used before): stone relief/chisel/bronze plaques (v10); linocut/gouge/ink roller/paper burn (v9); kalamkari cloth/stitches/dye drips (v8); sand-bottle strata/pearls (v7); shadow puppets/cracks (v6); blueprint/stamps/compass/measuring tape/balance scales/palm-leaf bundles (v5); lanterns going out (v4).
 - Captions are drawn by the engine at y ~1400-1640; keep y 1300-1760 calm (no big text there). Main action in y 150-1250.
 - Every shot: camera always moving (sh.zoom, sh.cam), something animates the whole time. Shots 1.5-4 s. Draw cost < ~0.2 s/frame: cache static tile layers with cached()/offscreen canvases; precompute tile arrays once (module-level lazy init).
